The Führer

Adolf Hitler

(20 April 1889 – 30 April 1945)

Adolf Hitler was the leader of Nazi Germany from 1933 to 1945. His regime established a totalitarian state built on aggressive nationalism, racial ideology, and expansionism. Under his leadership, Germany initiated World War II and carried out the Holocaust, resulting in the deaths of millions of people. His rule is widely regarded as one of the darkest periods in modern history due to the immense human suffering and destruction it caused.
